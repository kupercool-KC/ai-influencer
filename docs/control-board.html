What the quality gate looks for

Six families of defect, drawn from published research on temporal drift and AI-video detection. These are the failures that make a clip read as fake — and the ones audiences and platforms notice first.

FamilyWhat breaksOn failure
AnatomyFinger count and fusion, impossible joint bends, teeth distorting between frames, unnatural blinking, gaze driftRegenerate
Temporal driftFace morphing into a different person mid-clip, objects appearing or vanishing, background warping, frame-to-frame flickerRegenerate
IdentityFace no longer matches the persona reference; wardrobe or aesthetic off-brandRegenerate
PhysicsHair and fabric moving against the motion, shadows contradicting the light source, missing or wrong reflectionsFlag
Text & marksGarbled lettering on signs or clothing, accidental brand logos, leftover tool watermarksRegenerate
AudioLip-sync drift, audio running out of step with picture, wrong emphasis or robotic deliveryRegenerate
ComplianceWrong aspect ratio or duration, subject cropped by platform UI, missing AI disclosure, policy-breaking contentBlock

The one risk in the revenue model

Worth being precise about, because it decides whether the accounts survive. The pattern that gets creators banned is not a subscription link — it is a link to adult content. TikTok's guidelines prohibit adult-platform promotion outright, and Instagram has restricted bio links for creators it associates with adult platforms.

Low risk — where we are now

SFW lifestyle content on social, SFW exclusive content on Fanvue. This is structurally the same as a Patreon or Substack link, and it is a normal creator business.

Stay here and the accounts are not the thing at risk.

High risk — the line not to cross

SFW on social funnelling to NSFW on Fanvue is exactly the pattern platforms detect and act on. A link aggregator between the two is what the industry reaches for, but it mitigates rather than solves — the chain is still detectable.

If the Fanvue side ever goes NSFW, the social strategy needs rebuilding first.

The Berlin brief — historical, superseded by the 3-persona portfolio above

Nearly every AI influencer clusters in Miami, Dubai and LA: tanned, glossy, luxury-coded. Berlin is the opposite register — layered, monochrome, thrifted, effortful-looking only by accident. That distance is the point, and it happens to suit the app's realism prompts, which fight glamour and reward found light and real skin.

DimensionUseAvoid
VibeStreet, Cozy, CandidLuxury
WardrobeStreetwear, Minimal, CozyGlam
LocationsCoffee shop, city street, park, bedroom, mirror selfie, gymBeach — Berlin has lakes, not coastline

Luxury and Glam are the two presets that would collapse this persona back into the crowd. They stay off.
